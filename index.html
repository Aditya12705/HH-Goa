FAQ

questions & answers

Do I need an account to use this tool?
No. Zero accounts needed. The Frame Studio runs entirely in your browser — no sign-ups, no logins, no server-side processing. Just upload, customize, and download.
Is my photo uploaded to any server?
Never. All photo processing happens 100% on your device. Your image never leaves your browser. We don't have a server to upload to — the entire tool is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
Does this work on my phone?
Yes! The studio is built mobile-first. You can upload photos, adjust settings, and download your graphic directly on any modern smartphone browser.
Does making a frame give me a seat at HH Goa?
No — this is an Open Trials submission tool, not a ticket. To apply for one of the 247 seats, submit through the official Devfolio application.
What file formats are supported?
Upload any standard image: JPG, PNG, WebP, and even iPhone HEIC photos (automatically converted). The output is always a high-resolution PNG.
How fast is it?
Seconds from upload to shareable output. The canvas renders in real-time as you adjust controls. Download is instant — no processing queue, no waiting.
